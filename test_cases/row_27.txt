## Clinical Indications
Wrist CT scan may be indicated for 1 or more of the following:

Bone anatomy or structural defect evaluation needed, as indicated by 1 or more of the following:
- Assessment of calcinosis in systemic sclerosis when information is required for therapeutic management
- Avascular necrosis or osteonecrosis (eg, Kienbock disease), suspected

Cancer or neoplasm evaluation or staging needed

Infection, known or suspected

Pain, chronic, localized to wrist
