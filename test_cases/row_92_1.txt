Preoperative testing is considered **medically necessary** when ALL the following criteria are met (A, B, and C):

**A. Preoperative testing may include any, or all, of the following when done as part of a preoperative evaluation before low risk invasive procedures or non-cardiac surgeries:**

*   Comprehensive blood counts (CBC, WBC, PT/PTT, metabolic panel);
*   Urinalysis;
*   Chest X-rays;
*   Resting ECG;
*   Resting echocardiogram;
*   Pre-procedure consultations.

**and**

**B. Low risk invasive procedures and surgeries may include the following when criteria are met:**

*   Cataract surgery;
*   Glaucoma surgery;
*   Upper endoscopy;
*   Colonoscopy;
*   Cystoscopy;
*   Arthroscopy.

**and**

**C. Preoperative testing is considered medically necessary** for persons 65 years of age or older **or** for persons less than 65 years of age with risk factors for postoperative complications or symptoms suggestive of a significant systemic disease process (ASA III or IV*) when the same tests have not been performed in the previous 30 days **and** when **ANY** of the following conditions are present:

*   Anemia; or
*   Bleeding disorders; or
*   Other hematologic disorders; or
*   Cardiovascular disease; or
*   Pulmonary disease; or
*   Renal disease; or
*   Liver disease; or
*   Endocrine disease; or
*   Malignancy; or
*   Hypertension; or
*   Diabetes; or
*   Recent upper respiratory infection; or
*   History of smoking; or
*   History of alcohol abuse; or
*   History of steroid use; or
*   History of anticoagulant therapy.

*\*For definitions of American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classifications, see the Definitions section.*

Preoperative testing is considered **not medically necessary** when **ALL** of the following criteria are met:

*   Age between 16 and 65 years of age; **and**
*   When the same tests have been performed within 30 days prior to the planned procedure; **and**
*   Asymptomatic persons without significant systemic disease (ASA I or II*) of any system or risk factors for postoperative complications.
