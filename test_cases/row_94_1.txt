## Preoperative Evaluation of Asymptomatic Patients prior to Bariatric Surgery

Upper endoscopy is considered medically necessary for ANY of the following scenarios:

*   Suspected esophagitis or Barrett's esophagus prior to sleeve gastrectomy
*   Suspected gastric lesions prior to Roux-en-Y gastrojejunal bypass (RYGB) following H. pylori testing/treatment in ANY of the following high-risk patients:
    *   Age 55 years or older
    *   Positive H. pylori testing
    *   First-degree relative with history of gastric cancer
    *   Patients of racial or ethnic groups known to be at higher risk for gastric cancer
